Clinical scenario

Four consecutive lab visits (BMP, LFT, lipid panel) spanning ~12 months for one patient on long-term statin therapy, replayed end to end — ingestion, validation, FHIR persistence, then AI review. Most analytes stay in range throughout, so the interesting part is what the AI does with the two that don't.

Lipids improving on statin
ALT — transient, resolved
Glucose — new upward trend

Only records that pass schema and range validation are persisted as FHIR resources and reach the AI layer. Malformed or out-of-range rows are rejected at VALIDATE and written to the audit log — never silently dropped, never scored by the model.
